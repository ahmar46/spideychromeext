## Detailed description

A little hero. A healthier browsing rhythm.

Meet Web Buddy: a comic-style animated companion who hangs out in a corner of your webpages and helps you remember small breaks during a busy day.

Water, eye breaks, and a little room to stretch—without another account or noisy dashboard.

YOUR RHYTHM, YOUR RULES

• Choose water, eye-break, and stretch reminders from 1 to 240 minutes.
• Start a simple 20-second eye break when you are ready.
• Snooze a reminder for five minutes or pause your reminder clocks.
• See your completed breaks for the current day.

A BUDDY THAT FITS YOUR BROWSER

• A transparent character that drops in on a web, sways with articulated limb motion, then climbs away.
• Four corners, three sizes, and reduced-motion support.
• Keep your buddy visible or show him only for reminders.
• Hide him temporarily or disable him on individual websites.
• Change everything from a compact toolbar popup.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

No sign-in, ads, analytics, remote code, or external services. Settings and reminder counts stay in Chrome’s local extension storage. Web Buddy does not transmit data to its developer or third parties. It does not read your page text, form inputs, passwords, cookies, or browsing history.

TRY BEFORE YOU WAIT

Use the Water, Eyes, and Stretch test buttons to meet your reminders immediately. Test reminders do not change your real schedules or counts.

Web Buddy appears on regular HTTP and HTTPS webpages. Chrome does not allow it on internal pages or the Chrome Web Store. Refresh existing tabs after installation. Background alarms can be delayed while your computer sleeps. Web Buddy provides gentle reminders, not medical advice or activity tracking.

Stay human. Keep building.
